## Corrected — the lease P1 was real, and Chief ordered it rebutted twice

cubic filed a confidence-9 P1 on `factory#223`: an agent completing after its
lease expires can mark stale work complete and suppress re-admission for 30 days.
`factory-lead-0809` disputed it, Chief backed the dispute, and Chief then told the
successor lead to post that rebuttal on the thread. **Two leads were instructed to
reject a valid finding.**

Chief caught it only when re-reading its own argument before standing behind it at
the merge gate: the rebuttal said `claimId` fencing covers the case *once the
claim is re-admitted*, which is true and irrelevant — cubic's scenario is the
window **before** re-admission.

`factory-223-lead-0810` then settled it by execution, exit 0, and posted a public
correction on the thread with the numbers:

- `claimId` fencing only protects **after a replacement claim is minted**. Before
  that, lease expiry is just a timestamp — neither store changes status or
  `claimId` automatically, and `complete` does not check `leaseUntilMs`.
- The agent-exit path **completes before it triggers a sweep**.
- Lease is 15 minutes; the renewal sweep runs every 15 seconds — but it selects
  **routed-open-pr** claims. **Issue-created claims use a different owner and are
  not selected**, so they routinely outlive the lease.
- Completion is retained 30 days. Routed work is re-admitted on revision change;
  **issue-created completion suppresses re-admission for the whole period.**

The thread stays open by design: *"a bare lease check still risks duplicate
babysitting"*, so the correct transition semantics are being designed rather than
the reviewer's sentence being implemented.
