  Watching `lastReconcileAt` alone over two minutes showed no movement, which
  read as a dead timer. It was a backoff gap: the cursor did advance at
  14:04:24Z, and the real fault was that every attempt *failed*. The field that
  carries the meaning is `lastSuccessfulReconcileAt`, not `lastReconcileAt`.
  Right conclusion, wrong reason — worth naming, because the wrong reason
  pointed at a different fix.

  looked process-local. But linear resumed failing five minutes after the
  restart while digests kept succeeding, which is flapping resolution rather
  than one poisoned process. No credential or capacity work is involved. The
  practical consequence: senses freshness is unreliable per scope and per
